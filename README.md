# ChicStyle-Curator

ChicStyle Curator is a ResNet powered fashion recommendation system. It provides curated outfit suggestions based on user prompts (mood, occasion).It integrates personal wardrobe uploads for tailored recommendations and offers online product discovery with customizable store and price filters. Achieve your ideal style with precision and ease.
---

## 🚀 What It Does

ChicStyle Curator revolutionizes how you choose outfits. It provides smart, personalized fashion recommendations by:

* **Understanding Your Vibe:** Just type your mood or occasion (e.g., "sunny garden brunch," "midweek coffee with friends"), and it suggests the perfect look.
* **Integrating Your Wardrobe:** Upload pictures of your own clothes, and the system learns your style, recommending outfits directly from your closet.
* **Connecting You to New Finds:** Discover fresh pieces from your favorite online stores with direct product links.
* **Smart Shopping Filters:** Easily narrow down new product suggestions by preferred stores and price range.

---

## 🛠️ Technology Used 

* **Machine Learning:** Python with **ResNet CNNs** (for image analysis and feature extraction), relevant libraries (e.g., TensorFlow/PyTorch).
* **Natural Language Processing (NLP):** For interpreting text input (mood, occasion).
* **Data Storage:** For managing user wardrobes and product attributes.
* **API Integration:** For fetching product links from e-commerce platforms (conceptual/simulated for this project).
